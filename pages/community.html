The Pathfinders

A community for high school and university students who want to learn, grow, connect and build their futures together.

Community Activities

What Happens in the Pathfinders?

Weekly Reflections

Regular questions and prompts to help you think deeply about your journey, your goals and your growth.

Learning Resources

Curated articles, videos, guides and tools shared regularly to help you develop new skills and knowledge.

Career Discussions

Group conversations about different career paths, featuring stories and insights from professionals.

Opportunities

Scholarships, internships, competitions and other opportunities shared as they become available.

Challenges

Regular growth challenges that help you apply what you're learning and build real-world skills.

Mentor Sessions

Live sessions with mentors and professionals who share their experiences and answer your questions.

The Ecosystem

One Platform. Many Touchpoints.

The Pathways ecosystem connects physical engagements, online learning, community discussion and mentorship into one continuous journey.

Physical Engagements

Deep workshops, relationship-building and practical activities at schools.

Online Learning

Expert sessions, career talks and skills workshops via the Pathways platform.

WhatsApp Community

Continuous communication, resources, challenges and peer support.

Lead The Way

Become a Pathways Ambassador

Pathways Ambassadors are student leaders who champion the programme at their schools. They mobilise peers, share opportunities, encourage participation and promote a culture of growth.

  • Peer leadership and mobilisation
  • Share opportunities and resources
  • Support school engagement activities
  • Build leadership skills
  • Recognition and development
