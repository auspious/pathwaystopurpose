About Pathways to Purpose

Equipping Young People to Thrive

A year-round youth development, mentorship, career exposure and future-readiness programme under the Global Shapers Harare Hub.

Our Vision

A Generation of Purpose-Driven Leaders

A generation of young people who know who they are, understand their purpose, are prepared for the future, and confidently use their abilities to create meaningful impact in their communities.

Our Mission

Skills. Opportunities. Mindset.

To equip young people with skills, opportunities and a mindset that enable them to thrive academically, socially and career-wise — with particular emphasis on young people in marginalised communities and government schools who have limited access to mentorship, exposure and opportunity.

Our Anchor

Global Shapers Harare Hub

Pathways to Purpose is a project of the Global Shapers Harare Hub — part of the Global Shapers Community, an initiative of the World Economic Forum, comprising young people under 30 driving dialogue, action and change in their communities.

Programme Journey

Five Phases of Transformation

Phase 01

Personal Discovery & Mastery

Understanding yourself — strengths, values, interests, identity, confidence, and self-discipline as the essential foundation for your future.

Phase 02

Purpose, Vision & Direction

Exploring purpose, setting a clear vision, defining actionable goals, developing critical thinking, and making intentional decisions.

Phase 03

Employability & Future Readiness

Developing effective communication, emotional intelligence, digital literacy, practical AI skills, and professional readiness.

Phase 04

Exposure, Mentorship & Opportunity

Broadening horizons through career exploration, mentor connections, scholarships, internships, networking, and opportunity access.

Phase 05

Leadership & Community Impact

Stepping into active leadership, initiating community projects, serving others, and creating lasting positive social impact.

Our Commitment

Safeguarding

Pathways to Purpose serves young people, including minors. We are committed to creating a safe environment for all participants.

Our Principles

  • All mentor interactions are facilitated through the programme — never unsupervised.
  • Student personal data is handled with care and stored securely.
  • WhatsApp communities are moderated by the programme team.
  • We do not collect sensitive personal information unnecessarily.
  • We do not create public profiles of students.
  • We maintain clear communication boundaries between mentors and students.

Reporting

If you have any safeguarding concerns, please contact the Pathways to Purpose programme team through Global Shapers Harare Hub.

Data & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Pathways to Purpose respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any information you share with us.

What We Collect

For the current version of this platform, interactive tools (such as the self-discovery exercises) save data only in your browser's local storage. No personal data is transmitted to any server.

Forms

Contact forms collect only the information necessary to respond to your request. This may include name, email, school and message.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics at this time.

Your Rights

You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to remove any data saved by the interactive tools. If you have submitted information through a form, you may request its deletion by contacting us.
