People Like You Are Building Their Futures

These are not generic success stories. They are authentic journeys — with uncertainty, struggle, growth and purpose. Read them and know: if they can do it, so can you.

Technology · Career Journey

From Mufakose to Software Engineering

Growing up in Mufakose, I never imagined a career in technology was possible for someone like me. I didn't have a computer at home. I learned to code using borrowed phones and free Wi-Fi at a local library. Today, I work as a software developer — not because everything went perfectly, but because I refused to let my circumstances define my future.

"The biggest thing I learned was that starting from nothing doesn't mean you end with nothing. You just have to start."

Medicine · Self-Discovery

I Didn't Know What I Wanted to Become

At 16, everyone asked me what I wanted to be. I had no answer — and that made me feel like something was wrong with me. It wasn't until I started exploring my values and interests that I realised my passion for helping people could lead to a career in public health. Purpose came before the career title.

"Not knowing is not a weakness. It is the beginning of discovery."

Education · Mentorship

What I Wish I Knew Before University

I arrived at university thinking everything would fall into place automatically. It didn't. I struggled with time management, confidence and choosing the right path. Looking back, I wish I'd had someone to guide me — a mentor, a community, even just honest information about what to expect.

"University doesn't prepare you for everything. But knowing yourself before you get there makes a massive difference."

Entrepreneurship · Leadership

How I Turned My Passion Into a Career

I was always the one organising events, bringing people together and solving problems. But I didn't see that as a career skill — until someone showed me that leadership and event management were real professions. Today I run a small events business while finishing my degree.

"Sometimes the career you're looking for doesn't exist yet. You have to build it yourself."
