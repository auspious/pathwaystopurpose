Where Will Your Path Lead?

A career is only one part of your journey — but it's an important one. Explore different career paths, understand what they involve, and discover what subjects and skills can help you get there.

Career Categories

Discover What's Possible

Click on a career area to explore what it involves, what skills are needed, and how to get started. Qualifications and pathways may vary by country and institution.

Technology

Software development, data science, cybersecurity, AI, web development, IT

Key subjects: Mathematics, Computer Science, Physics

Key skills: Problem solving, logical thinking, coding, creativity

Medicine & Health

Doctors, nurses, pharmacists, public health, biomedical science, mental health

Key subjects: Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics

Key skills: Empathy, attention to detail, communication, resilience

Engineering

Civil, mechanical, electrical, chemical, mining, environmental engineering

Key subjects: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry

Key skills: Problem solving, analytical thinking, design, teamwork

Law

Legal practice, human rights, corporate law, policy, governance, judiciary

Key subjects: English, History, Social Studies

Key skills: Critical thinking, writing, public speaking, research

Finance & Business

Accounting, banking, consulting, management, marketing, economics

Key subjects: Mathematics, Accounting, Business Studies

Key skills: Analytical thinking, communication, leadership, numeracy

Creative Industries

Design, film, music, fashion, photography, content creation, architecture

Key subjects: Art, Media Studies, English, ICT

Key skills: Creativity, self-discipline, digital literacy, storytelling

Education

Teaching, training, curriculum development, educational technology, research

Key subjects: Your area of passion + Education studies

Key skills: Communication, patience, creativity, leadership

Agriculture & Environment

Agribusiness, conservation, food science, environmental management, forestry

Key subjects: Biology, Chemistry, Geography, Agriculture

Key skills: Research, problem solving, sustainability thinking

Science & Research

Research, biotechnology, chemistry, physics, space science, geology

Key subjects: Sciences, Mathematics

Key skills: Analytical thinking, curiosity, patience, precision

Entrepreneurship

Starting businesses, social enterprise, innovation, startups, freelancing

Key subjects: Business, ICT, any area of passion

Key skills: Resilience, creativity, financial literacy, networking

Government & Public Service

Policy, diplomacy, civil service, international development, public administration

Key subjects: History, Economics, Social Studies, Law

Key skills: Leadership, communication, empathy, analytical thinking

Social Impact

NGOs, community development, social work, humanitarian, advocacy, human rights

Key subjects: Social Studies, Economics, Languages

Key skills: Empathy, communication, project management, resilience

Career pathways and qualifications vary by country, institution and industry. Always research specific requirements for your context.
