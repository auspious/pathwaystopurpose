For Schools

Bring Pathways to Your School

We partner with government high schools to deliver youth development workshops, career exposure, mentorship and student leadership programmes. Our priority is schools where students have the least access to these opportunities.

What We Offer

Programme Activities for Schools

Physical Workshops

Deep, interactive workshops on self-discovery, purpose, career readiness and leadership delivered in-person at your school.

Online Sessions

Regular virtual sessions covering expert talks, career stories, skills workshops and interactive learning.

Mentorship

Connect students with professional mentors who share career guidance and life experience.

Career Exposure

Career talks, industry visits and the annual Pathways Career Fair to broaden students' horizons.

Student Ambassadors

Train peer leaders at your school to champion growth, share opportunities and sustain engagement.

Digital Learning

Access to Pathways Academy — structured courses, resources and challenges available on any device.
